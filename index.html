User story & acceptance criteriaRepresentative excerpt · anonymized
US-104

As a customer using the app, I want to track the status of my request, so that I don't need to call support.

Given a submitted request, when I open "My requests", then I see its current status and the time of the last update.
Given the status changes in the back-office system, when the sync runs, then I receive a notification within 5 minutes.
